How we work

Small steps, visible results

You see something working on your own data early, before committing to a larger build.

  1. Discovery call

    A free 30-minute call about your goal, your data and what a good result looks like.

  2. Prototype

    A working prototype on a sample of your data, so you can judge the answers yourself.

  3. Build and integrate

    The solution is hardened, tested and connected to your tools and access rules.

  4. Support and improve

    We measure answer quality over time and improve it as your data and needs change.

Principles

Private

Practical

About

Founded in Aachen

ZenrAiqi is founded by Rahul Gupta, who studied Data Analytics and Decision Science at RWTH Aachen University.

His master's research focused on AI agents for process intelligence and decision support: systems that combine company data, process analysis and document search to give answers that can be checked.

The name joins Zen (clarity), Rai (Japanese for thunder, energy) and qi (flow), with Ai at its centre.
